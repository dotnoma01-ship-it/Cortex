## Listing A24: readable evidence and limits

Source: [Alibaba listing 1600549156411](https://www.alibaba.com/product-detail/High-Gloss-Plastic-Sheets-Panel-De_1600549156411.html). The page showed “High Gloss Plastic Sheets Panel … PVC Marble Sheet”, supplier Guangzhou Titan Commercial Co., Ltd., model “PVC Marble Sheet”, material PVC, size 1220 × 2440 mm, thickness 2–5 mm, UV coating, wall decoration use, and composition listed as 30% PVC, 67% CaCO3, 3% additive. The page showed supplier tiers of USD 9.80 (200–999 pieces), USD 9.20 (1,000–4,999), USD 8.70 (5,000–9,999), and USD 7.50 (10,000+); the lowest displayed tier begins at 200, but a distinct minimum order quantity was not confirmed. Shipping fee and delivery date were stated as negotiable. A product gallery was present, but stable direct image URLs, variant-to-image matching, and rights to reuse those images were not verified.

Classification: related PVC marble-effect panel; safe to retain as an internal research lead, not safe for the regular catalogue. Exact selectable thickness/patterns, current quote, formal MOQ, shipping destination terms, Cortex availability, approved retail price and permitted stable image remain unresolved. Listing assertions about performance are supplier claims, not independently confirmed.
